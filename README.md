# Critical AI Case Studies

Critical AI Case Studies is a collection of hands-on machine learning cases for teaching critical AI literacy in business education. It is designed for instructors who can read and adapt Python notebooks and who teach business students or professionals with little or no programming experience.

The cases give participants a look at the work that goes into building a machine learning system. They explore and prepare data, train and test a model, and read its outputs. The code is prewritten and visible throughout. Instructors can use it to explain what happens at each step, and participants can change selected inputs or settings and see how the results respond.

## Teaching with the cases

The lab uses the modelling process itself to teach critical evaluation. Participants work with a model before assessing what it has learned, where its errors matter, and what it offers over a simpler approach. They use evidence from the notebook to explain their conclusions and discuss the implications of using the system in practice.

The business scenarios give participants a setting in which to interpret the results. Instructors can adapt the discussion to their course, whether the focus is machine learning, a particular business discipline, or AI ethics and governance.

## Cases

Cases are organized by business discipline. Open the folder for the discipline you teach and start with its README to find the cases and their materials.

The student materials include the scenario, guided notebook, dataset or instructions for obtaining it, and discussion questions. The instructor materials explain how to prepare and facilitate the lab, how the case was designed, and what to look for in the model outputs.

## Contributing a case

This repository is open to collaboration. You can propose a case in another business discipline, adapt an existing case for a different cohort, or improve the notebooks and teaching materials.

Start with the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [case creation guide](templates/CASE_CREATION_GUIDE.md). The [case template](templates/case-template/) provides a structure for the scenario, student notebook, dataset, discussion questions, and instructor notes.

When developing a case, document where the data comes from, how you prepared it, and how another instructor can run the notebook. Explain what you want participants to discover and which outputs or experiments help them investigate it. That gives other instructors enough context to decide how to teach or adapt the case.
